The daily structure

Mornings for the mind. Afternoons for the game.

Academics run from 8 AM to 1 PM every weekday. From 1 PM onward, student-athletes shift to sport-specific training with their coaching staff.

This structure means athletes get full academic preparation without sacrificing training volume. No more choosing between school and sport.
