Academic Pathway

Education + Elite Development

Level Up Prep Academy is designed to bring serious student athletes into one development environment while allowing families to use participating education providers for their academic program.

Academics
Education Provider

Students complete their academic coursework through the education provider selected by their family, subject to that provider’s enrollment requirements and policies.

Level Up Prep
Student Athlete Environment

Level Up Prep provides the in-person development environment including basketball training, performance, study hall, accountability, film and team preparation.

Competition
Basketball Pathway

Student athletes can compete through age and grade appropriate Level Up teams and planned Prep basketball teams as the program expands.

Education Options

Participating Education Providers

Families may have education options through the following providers. Enrollment, funding eligibility, curriculum and program requirements are determined by each education provider.

Mission Vista AcademyEducation provider option for eligible participating families.
BEST AcademyEducation provider option for eligible participating families.
Pacific Coast AcademyEducation provider option for eligible participating families.
EPIC California AcademyEducation provider option for eligible participating families.

Education providers are separate organizations. Availability, enrollment, vendor funding and eligibility are subject to each provider’s current policies. Families should confirm academic enrollment and available educational funding directly with their selected provider.

Grades 6 Through 8

Middle School Prep

Middle school student athletes are part of Level Up Prep Academy. Competition will be organized through age and grade appropriate teams, club and AAU opportunities, independent games and other eligible competition.

8th Grade
Prepare for high school

Advanced development, strength and performance, basketball IQ, film study and high school preparation.
